Paint the world you've seen.

Wanderpaint reads only the place and date of your photos, right on your iPhone, and paints every country, US state, Japanese prefecture and Canadian province you've been to — each with the day you first went.

What it does

  • Scan your photo library once and your map fills in: countries, 267 states, provinces and prefectures in 12 countries, first and latest visit.
  • Or tap countries by hand: Been here, Lived here, Want to go, with dates and notes.
  • Stats: countries out of 197, UN members out of 193, share of the world's land, progress per continent, new countries by year.
  • Pro: region maps, every trip rebuilt from your photos, deeper stats and all poster styles. One-time purchase or yearly.

Help & questions

My photos didn't find a country. Only photos with a location can be placed. Photos taken with Location turned off for the Camera, or received from other people without location, are skipped. You can always tap the country on the map.

It found a country I only flew through. On the review screen, uncheck it. Or open the country and tap “Been here” again to clear it. Wanderpaint remembers and won't add it back on the next scan.

New iPhone? Your map syncs through your own iCloud account (Settings › Sync with iCloud). Purchases come back with Settings › Restore purchases.

Why these borders? Borders come from Natural Earth, a public-domain dataset that shows borders as they are on the ground. Disputed places are listed as countries or territories the way most travellers count them.
