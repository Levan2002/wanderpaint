Wanderpaint · Privacy policy · updated 1 October 2026

Your map is yours.

Photos

When you choose to scan your library, Wanderpaint reads the location and capture date of your photos and videos on your iPhone to work out which countries and regions you visited. It does not read the image contents, never uploads photos or their locations, and never changes your library.

Your travel log

The places, dates, notes and trips you keep are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud sync is on (you can turn it off in Settings), a copy is kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's key-value storage so your other devices can see it. The developer can't access it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Wanderpaint uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro purchase. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (products, dates, prices) for this app. This data is used only to provide the purchase, is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase my map removes everything from your iPhone and your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the on-device data.
